About PedalPro

Founded in 2025, PedalPro has been serving the cycling community with expert bicycle repair and cleaning services. Our mission is to keep cyclists safe and their bikes performing at their best.

Our certified mechanics have over 5 years of combined experience working with all types of bicycles - from road bikes to mountain bikes, kids bikes to even street bikes.
